Why Sovereign Key Vault exists

I'm Dale Williams. I founded Sovereign Key Vault because most regulated firms encrypt their data, yet few can show in writing who holds the key and what a demand on their provider could produce.

A UK data centre tells you where a server stands, not which companies can be ordered to act. I give boards a written answer to that question, then help them act on it.

Evidence, not claims

Every answer is scored twice: once for the strength of the control, and once for the evidence behind it. A control you can prove scores higher than one you can only describe, and a structural gap cannot hide behind good paperwork.
