How it works

From question to proof

An assessment first. Then, if you choose, the work to fix what it finds and keep it fixed.

Three steps

1

Assessment

A fixed-fee written report on who generates, stores and can use each key that protects your data. It covers 48 questions in eight areas, each backed by evidence you supply.

2

Implementation

Most reports point to one action first: move the master key for your most sensitive data into certified hardware you control. A first wave connects up to eight systems, so the provider must call a service you control before it can decrypt.

3

Managed key custody

Your keys stay in a dedicated hardware module hosted in the UK, from a vendor and host checked for any US parent. Each month you receive a report of every operation requested on your keys.

21 working days, start to finish

Your part is one senior sponsor, one architect and a folder of documents you already hold. The stages overlap, so a delay in one does not stop the others.

  1. Day 0 Scoping call We confirm data, systems and providers.
  2. Days 1 to 5 Questionnaire You answer 48 questions in your own words.
  3. Days 3 to 10 Evidence review You send exports with secrets removed.
  4. Days 8 to 12 Interview A 90-minute session with your architect.
  5. Days 12 to 15 Scoring A second consultant checks every score.
  6. Days 17 to 21 Report and read-out We walk through it in 60 minutes.
